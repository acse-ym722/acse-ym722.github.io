> This is a reusable outline. Copy this file, replace its metadata and content, and start a new paper reading note.

## One-sentence summary

State the problem the paper addresses and its most important conclusion in one sentence.

## Research context

Keep only the background needed to understand the paper. Define the specific problem first, then explain why existing approaches are insufficient.

## Core method

Describe the method through its inputs, process, and outputs. Break complex equations or architectures into smaller questions.

## Key evidence

List the experiments or results that genuinely support the conclusion. Separate the evidence presented, the authors' interpretation, and your own judgment.

## Limitations and questions

Record uncovered scenarios, underlying assumptions, and questions the paper leaves unanswered.

## What I take away

Explain how the paper affects your research: a reusable method, a new formulation, or an idea worth testing next.
